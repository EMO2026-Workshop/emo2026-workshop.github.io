THE WORKSHOP

Connecting research.
Sharing what comes next.

EMO2026 brings together researchers working on electromagnetic modeling, microwave technologies and electronic design automation. The program explores how AI and advanced modeling methods are shaping the design of devices, circuits and systems.

Two keynote talks and ten invited talks are followed by an evening session with short presentations and discussion with senior professionals.
